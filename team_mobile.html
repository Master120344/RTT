Who We Are

A Team Built on Compassion, Service, and Faith

Redeeming The Time Today is made up of people who care deeply about the community and believe that real change happens when support is personal, consistent, and rooted in love.

Our team is dedicated to walking with individuals and families through difficult seasons by offering prayer, mentorship, outreach, practical care, and encouragement that reflects both compassion and commitment.

We are not here just to organize programs. We are here to serve people, build trust, and help create an environment where healing, dignity, and second chances can grow.

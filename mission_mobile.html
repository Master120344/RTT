Our Mission

Called to Bring Hope, Healing, and Restoration

Redeeming The Time Today exists to serve individuals and families with compassion, truth, prayer, practical support, mentorship, and consistent encouragement during life’s hardest seasons.

Get Help Support The Mission

Why We Exist

We Believe Every Life Has Value

At Redeeming The Time Today, we believe no person is beyond hope, no family is beyond healing, and no difficult season has the final word. Our mission is rooted in the belief that people deserve compassion, dignity, support, and a real opportunity to move forward.

We are committed to meeting people where they are with love, consistency, prayer, and practical help. Whether someone is facing brokenness, transition, hardship, isolation, addiction recovery, reentry challenges, spiritual battles, or family instability, we want them to know they are not forgotten.

Our goal is not simply to speak about hope. Our goal is to live it out through action, service, presence, and a community that stands beside people while they rebuild.

What We Stand By

Core Principles That Guide Everything We Do

These values shape the way we serve, lead, support, and care for our community.

01

Dignity for Every Person

We believe every individual deserves to be treated with respect, compassion, and honor regardless of their past, their struggles, or their present circumstances.

02

Truth With Compassion

We believe real transformation happens when truth is shared with grace. We aim to encourage people honestly while still surrounding them with love and patience.

03

Restoration Over Rejection

We stand for second chances, healing, reconciliation, and restoration. We do not believe a person’s worst moment should define their whole life.

04

Faith in Action

Our mission is lived out through prayer, service, outreach, encouragement, and practical support that reaches real needs in the community.

05

Consistency Matters

Lasting impact is built over time. We believe people need more than a moment of inspiration. They need steady support, accountability, and ongoing care.

06

Community Changes Lives

Healing and growth are stronger when people are supported by a caring community. We believe no one should have to walk through hardship alone.

What We Believe

We Believe in Redemption

We believe God can restore what has been broken, strengthen what has been weakened, and renew what feels lost. We believe hope is still possible. We believe healing can happen. We believe prayer matters. We believe families can be strengthened. We believe people can grow, change, and begin again.

We believe compassion should be visible, practical, and sincere. That means listening, helping, encouraging, serving, and standing with people through real life situations.

The Heart Behind The Work

More Than A Program — A Calling

Redeeming The Time Today is more than an organization. It is a commitment to show compassion in practical ways, to stand beside people when life gets heavy, and to create a place where hope is spoken, demonstrated, and shared.

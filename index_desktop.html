Our October 2026 focus

A little light goes a long way.

The evenings get a little longer. The small acts of care matter just as much.

A thought for the season

Picture a neighbor coming home after a hard day. The street is getting dark, but someone has left a porch light on and saved a place at the table. The day has not suddenly become easy. Still, that small welcome says: you matter, and you do not have to carry everything alone.

We can be that welcome for one another. This October, make the call you have been putting off. Share a meal. Offer a prayer, then help with the next practical step. A little light can be enough to help someone begin again.

“Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father which is in heaven.”

Matthew 5:16 · King James Version

Who We Are

Building Stronger Futures Together

Redeeming The Time Today was founded with a simple belief that no one is too far gone and every life still has value, purpose, and potential.

Through outreach, encouragement, mentorship, and practical support, we help people move forward with dignity, hope, and renewed direction.

Our Mission

What Guides Us

To provide hope, healing, and practical support to individuals and families facing difficult seasons of life. We meet people where they are and walk with them toward restoration and purpose.
